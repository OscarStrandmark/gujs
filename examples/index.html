Shakshuka

Serves 4 · 35 minutes

Ingredients

  • 30 ml olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 2 red bell peppers, sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tsp smoked paprika
  • 800 g crushed tomatoes
  • 6 eggs
  • 100 g feta, crumbled
  • 15 g fresh coriander

Steps

  1. Heat the oil in a wide pan and soften the onion and peppers for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin and paprika and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Pour in the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes until thick.
  4. Make wells in the sauce, crack in the eggs, cover and cook for 6 minutes.
  5. Top with feta and coriander and serve from the pan.
